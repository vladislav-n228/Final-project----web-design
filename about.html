The making of Galaxy Orbit from a simple project to Steam

Chapter 1 January 2025

A morning insight, a random background and a change of course

It all started early in the morning on Thursday, January 8, 2025. At first the developer had a completely different idea: the game was meant to be about something else, and the concept felt quite ordinary. But everything changed in one moment, when the author accidentally saw the main menu background of the future game. This picture was so mesmerizing and set such a strong mood that it instantly turned the whole project around and moved it to a new, unexplored theme: endless space. Thanks to this insight the game became the space project we know today.

Before that moment, many different experiments and ideas had been tried. After giving up on an unsuccessful attempt with shared-code software, development finally moved to classic VS Code, where a new pixel 2D sandbox about the endless universe was born.

Chapter 2 February – May 2025

A school website, the SEGA logo and burnout

In February the developer made a special website for computer science lessons, describing the game's concept and its genre (sci-fi and fantasy) in detail. A fun detail of that time: when the game started, it temporarily showed someone else's logo, SEGA. The author knew it was only temporary, until the project had its own brand. Later the game got its own studio logo: Bloom.

But development ran into difficulties. An attempt to add complex multiplayer led to strong emotional burnout, and work stopped for 1–2 months: from the end of February until May 3.

Chapter 3 May 2025

The comeback and teasers

Putting multiplayer on hold and focusing on single-player mode, the author came back to the code with new energy. This productive stage brought two teaser trailers:

  • May 8 — the first teaser
  • May 16 — the second teaser
Chapter 4 2026

Going international and the road to Steam

After an early version was released on Itch.io and many updates followed, the project grew far beyond a school experiment. On August 26, 2026 the official Steam developer fee was paid, opening the way to a full release.

Before that, the game went through a big optimization and got full Xbox gamepad support. Now, with the deep economy system (tycoon genre) finished for version v0.1.13, the game is getting ready for its Steam release and the autumn demo festival under the Bloom studio.
